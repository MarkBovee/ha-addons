## HA MCP findings: where the helper sensors come from
- sensor.current_price = UI template helper: picks the price of the current quarter from the price_curve attribute of sensor.energy_prices_electricity_import_price. Used by dashboard and automations (airco on < 0.10, airco off at high prices, kachel/switch.ems on < 0).
- State of sensor.energy_prices_electricity_import_price (0.2648 at 08:04) does NOT equal the current quarter in its own price_curve (0.4513). Cause unknown.
- sensor.power_usage = template: ((production * -1) + consumption) * 1000 from sensor.electricity_meter_power_production/consumption (kW) -> W, negative = export.
- sensor.average_power_usage = filter helper, 1-minute simple moving average of sensor.power_usage.
- sensor.energy_production_today_remaining = Solar production forecast integration, Wh.
